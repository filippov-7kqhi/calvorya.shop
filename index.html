About Calvorya

Mechanics Before Max Effort

Founded by former minor league infielder Nash Delgado, Calvorya was built on a simple belief: swinging harder or throwing harder without sound mechanics just builds bad habits faster. Every program is built on fundamentals first.

  • ✓ Coaches with pro and collegiate playing experience
  • ✓ Video-reviewed swing and pitching mechanics
  • ✓ Programs for youth league through travel ball athletes
  • ✓ Baseball and fastpitch softball tracks
